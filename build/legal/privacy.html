Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This is a draft placeholder. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must not be treated as binding legal text until it is.

1. What we collect

To create an account and process orders, we collect:

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed, never in plain text).
  • Order details: delivery address, order history, meal plan preferences.
  • Payment metadata: our payment provider (PayFast) handles card details directly — we never see or store your full card number.
  • Device and usage data: basic diagnostic information needed to keep the app secure and working.

2. Why we collect it

We use your data to create and secure your account, process and deliver orders, manage meal plan subscriptions, respond to support requests, and meet legal and tax obligations.

3. Legal basis and your rights (POPIA)

We process personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us via Help & Support.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what's necessary with: our database and authentication provider (Supabase), our payment provider (PayFast), and delivery drivers (name, delivery address, and phone number only, for the purpose of completing your order). We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data security

Access to your data is protected by authentication and Row Level Security policies at the database level, so only you — and staff who need it to fulfil your order — can access your records. Traffic to and from the app is encrypted (HTTPS).

6. Data retention

We keep account and order data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet accounting, tax, and dispute-resolution obligations. You can request deletion of your account at any time via Help & Support.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in between visits. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent via Help & Support or WhatsApp on 082 327 6367.
